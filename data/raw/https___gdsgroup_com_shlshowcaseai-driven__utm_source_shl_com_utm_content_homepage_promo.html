Talent Intelligence in an AI-driven World

AI is no longer a future consideration for HR.
It is already reshaping how people are assessed, hired, developed, and led.

  • Can AI be trusted in talent decisions?

  • How do we reskill and redeploy people fast enough to keep pace with change?

  • What talent data do we need — and can we trust it?

  • What does effective leadership look like in an AI-first world?

As generative and agentic AI accelerate, HR leaders are facing urgent, high-stakes questions:

This Virtual Summit explores Talent Intelligence as the foundation for confident, defensible decision-making in an AI-accelerated world. We’ll focus on the real challenges organisations are grappling with today - from assessment integrity and future skills planning to leadership readiness and workforce transformation - and how Talent Intelligence turns fragmented data into coherent action.

Drawing on real-world examples and SHL’s approach to transparent, responsible, and scalable AI, we’ll examine how organisations can connect and reuse talent data across the lifecycle - from acquisition to management, combining internal and third-party insights to build a trusted, future-ready view of skills, potential, and risk.

We’re delighted to be joined by Gartner, sharing their latest insights on what’s keeping CHROs awake in 2026.
